Signal log and backtest

Every Setup armed and Entry zone the page showed is saved and scored against what price did next: Target (reached VWAP first), Stop (hit the stop first; both in one minute counts as Stop), Time-out (neither in 30 minutes, scored in R at that point) or Not filled (never traded at the limit within 2 bars). Compare Taken with Lapsed and Skipped to see whether hesitation or skipping costs you money.

Replay runs the same detector over about the last 60 days of 1-minute data (days with 10+ earlier sessions), one stock at a time. Re-running replaces that stock's replay rows. No live quote exists in a replay, so the spread and circuit checks are skipped.
